§ 00 — The premise

How unlikely is unlikely?

Odds are just ratios wearing a disguise. Most of us are bad at reading them once the numbers get large or the events get small. What follows is a working almanac: a handful of everyday improbabilities, a simulator that shows you exactly how much a short run of luck can lie, and a scale long enough to hold the extremes.

§ 01 — The Board

Eight ways the world surprises you.

Each card is stated the honest way: one favourable outcome against the total. The bar underneath is the same probability compressed onto a logarithmic length, so you can compare them at a glance.

Figures are ballpark estimates from commonly cited sources and vary with assumptions — skill level, geography, house rules, how you count a leap year. Treat them as shapes, not as promises.
