Method

Figures are order-of-magnitude estimates drawn from astronomy, demography and one very specific geography: Sherman, Texas and North Fort Worth, Texas.

Where precision was impossible, the number was rounded generously in the direction of humility.

Conclusion

Under every reasonable assumption, the null hypothesis should have held: two people, 105 miles apart, in a state of 30 million, on a planet of 8 billion, in a universe of 200 sextillion stars, would not meet.

It did not hold.
